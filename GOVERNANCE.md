# Dusk project governance

This document says who owns Dusk and who decides. It is a policy, not a
license: the license is [LICENSE](LICENSE) and the terms on which
contributions are accepted are
[the contributor agreement](docs/docs/legal/cla.md). Where this document
and either of those disagree, the license and the agreement win.

## Ownership

Dusk has one owner: the copyright holder named in [README.md](README.md).
The owner holds the copyright in the project, and every contribution to it
is assigned to the owner under the agreement. Contributing to Dusk gives
nobody any ownership of the project, or any right in it beyond what the
license and the agreement grant.

## Decisions

The owner decides every question about the project: what is in scope,
what is merged and released, who may review, merge or release, the
license, the agreement, and this document. The owner may delegate any of
this and take it back. There is no vote, no committee, and no process by
which this changes.

Decisions are made in the open, in an issue, a pull request or a commit
message in this repository. A decision that is not written down there was
not made.

## Changes to the tree

Nothing lands on `master` except through a pull request, with the
agreement signed by every account that authored a commit on it, merged by
the owner or by someone the owner has delegated that to. History on
`master` is not rewritten. Third-party material enters the tree only under
a license compatible with the project's, in its own directory with its
license file beside it, and is never edited in place.

## Conduct

Everyone taking part in the project treats the others with professional
respect. Disagreement is about the work, never the person. The owner may
remove a comment, close a thread or block an account.

## Security

Vulnerabilities are handled as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) says.

## Succession

The owner may name a successor. Once the successor agrees in writing to
the obligations of the agreement, as its section 6.3 requires, the
project, this document and every agreement under it pass to the successor
together.
